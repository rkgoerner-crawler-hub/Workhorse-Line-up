Where do I find my league link?

ESPN: open your team on fantasy.espn.com in a web browser (desktop mode works on a phone) and copy the address. It contains leagueId=. The league must be viewable to the public: League, then Settings, Edit Settings, Basic Settings, Make League Viewable to Public. Only the league manager can change it, and it lets people see rosters and scores, nothing more.

Sleeper: open your league at sleeper.com and copy the address (sleeper.com/leagues/...). Sleeper leagues work as they are.

            How points are figured

            Projected pts is the number from your fantasy site. ROS avg is the points per week you expect for the rest of the season, carry bonus included; leave it blank to reuse this week's number. Players added from the database get it filled in. Carries only matter if your site's projection leaves out the carry bonus. Tick "Started" on the players in your real lineup to see what you are leaving on the bench.

                Scoring

                Added to each player's projected points: bonus = carries × this number. If your site's projections already include the carry bonus, set carries to 0 or this to 0.

                Starting slots

                Set these to match your league. FLEX takes RB, WR or TE.

                Chance of playing

                Percent chance a player suits up at each injury status. A player on bye is always 0. Projected points are multiplied by this.

                Season

                Waivers and Trade add up your best lineup from the current week through the last week counted. Set it to the last week of your regular season or playoffs. Roster spots decides when a pickup or trade forces a drop.
